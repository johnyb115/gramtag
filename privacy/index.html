Privacy policy

This policy describes how Gramtag (formerly Portionix) handles your data: the Android app, the web version at gramtag.eu/app, and this website.

In short: your settings, history and freezer stay on your device. Gramtag has no account and no analytics. Only if you choose to share your freezer with another phone is the freezer stored online, as described under Freezer sharing.

What the app stores

To work, Gramtag saves the following on your device:

  • the names you give the people you cook for (optional; “Person 1” and “Person 2” by default),
  • your foods, portion sizes and cooking factors, and your pans and their weights,
  • your calculation history and what you have in the freezer,
  • your choices for colours, light or dark theme, language and freezer reminders, and a count of your calculations (used only to ask once for a rating, see Ratings).

Gramtag itself does not collect any other personal information, location or contacts.

The web version

In your browser, Gramtag keeps the same data in your browser’s storage on your device; nothing is sent to a server unless you share your freezer, which works as described under Freezer sharing. Reminders are not available on the web. Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes it.

This website

gramtag.eu is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub receives technical data such as your IP address to deliver it, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads its fonts from gramtag.eu itself. If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is remembered in your browser.

Freezer sharing (optional)

You can share your freezer with other phones or browsers, for example your partner’s, using “Share this freezer” and “Join with a code” in the Freezer tab. Nothing is sent anywhere until you do. When you share or join, the following is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany):

  • the contents of your freezer: meal names, the date they were frozen, what is in the boxes, portions and grams, and the names of the people the portions are for, as you entered them;
  • a random identifier that Firebase Authentication creates for your phone or browser (anonymous sign-in). It is not linked to your name, email address, Google account or phone number;
  • one-time codes for joining, which expire after 24 hours.

This data is used only to show the same freezer on the phones that share it. Only phones and browsers that joined with a code can read or change it. Your settings and calculation history are never shared. While handling these requests, Google also receives technical data such as your IP address. Google processes the data on the developer’s behalf under the Firebase data processing terms; see also Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Feedback

“Send feedback” in the app opens an email to the developer. If you send it, the developer receives your email address, your message, and the app version, platform and language that the app adds below it. They are used only to reply to you and to improve Gramtag, and are deleted on request.

Ratings

After a few calculations, the Android app may ask Google Play once to show its rating dialog. Whether it appears is up to Google. A rating or review you give is handled by Google Play under Google’s Privacy Policy; the developer sees reviews only as Google Play publishes them.

Advertising and purchases

Gramtag currently shows no advertising. The app contains switched-off code for ads from Google AdMob and for a one-time Google Play purchase to remove them. If ads are ever switched on, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to show and measure ads, under Google’s Privacy Policy, and this policy will be updated first. Purchases are processed by Google Play; the developer receives no payment details. Gramtag does not sell or rent your data, and shares nothing with third parties other than what is described here.

Backups

Backup files. With “Save backup” in Settings you can save your settings, history and freezer as a file (JSON) in a place you choose, and restore it later with “Restore from backup”. Only you decide where this file is kept or who you share it with.

Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may include the app’s data in your device backup to your Google account, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy; the developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

Notifications and the screen

If you turn on freezer reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone. They are created on the device; no server is involved. While the full-screen plating view is open, the app keeps the screen on; this uses no data.

Deleting your data

You can delete history entries and freezer items in the app, and reset all settings in Settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings removes all of its data from the device; on the web, clear this site’s data in your browser.

Shared freezer. Tap “Stop sharing” in the Freezer tab: your phone leaves the shared freezer and keeps its own copy. When the last phone stops sharing, the shared freezer is deleted from the server. Uninstalling the app does not delete a shared freezer, so stop sharing first, or ask for deletion at the contact below (mention a meal in the freezer and the date it was frozen, so it can be found).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your data stored for freezer sharing or sent with feedback, and you can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in the Czech Republic: ÚOOÚ).

Children

Gramtag is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.
